[TC-01] Working Directory & Asset Loading
- Preconditions: Game files are located in projet/, terminal is opened from a parent directory.
- Steps: Run python projet/main.py from outside the projet/ folder.
- Expected Result: os.chdir() sets the working directory to projet/, all .gif shapes (pinguin.gif, fish.gif, fence.gif, etc.) register without TclError, and the window opens.

[TC-02] 4-Racer Setup & Alignment
- Preconditions: Game is initialized via initialize().
- Steps:
  1. Observe the starting positions of all racers on the track.
  2. Verify the number of spawned food items and obstacles.
- Expected Result: 4 racers (Monkey, Dog, TurtleRacer, Pinguin) appear at x = -450 on Y-lanes 75, 25, -25, -75. 10 colored zones, 9 food items, and 9 fences spawn.

[TC-03] Pinguin Terrain Speed Adjustment
- Preconditions: Race is in progress; Pinguin is at default speed (1) on "white" terrain.
- Steps:
  1. Observe Pinguin entering a "light blue" (water) area.
  2. Observe Pinguin entering a "light green" (forest) area.
  3. Observe Pinguin returning to "white" terrain.
- Expected Result:
  1. Speed increases by +1 (to 2).
  2. Speed decreases by -0.25.
  3. Speed resets to 1.

[TC-04] Fish Consumption (Pinguin Boost)
- Preconditions: Fish item is spawned in Pinguin's lane (y = -75).
- Steps: Let Pinguin move within < 10 units (X and Y) of a Fish object.
- Expected Result: Pinguin's speed doubles (speed *= 2), the Fish sprite hides (food.hide()), and it is removed from foods.

[TC-05] Food Species Exclusivity
- Preconditions: Fish is spawned in Dog's lane (y = 25), or Banana is in Pinguin's lane (y = -75).
- Steps: Let an animal pass directly over a food item belonging to another species.
- Expected Result: No speed boost occurs, and the food item remains visible on the track.

[TC-06] Fence Collision (obstacle_hit)
- Preconditions: Fence obstacle is spawned in any animal's lane.
- Steps: Let any racer (Monkey, Dog, TurtleRacer, or Pinguin) move within < 10 units of a Fence.
- Expected Result: The colliding animal's speed is halved (speed *= 0.5), the Fence hides (obstacle.hide()), and it is removed from obstacles.

[TC-07] Dog Terrain Reset Behavior
- Preconditions: Dog hits a Fence (speed = 0.5) or eats a Bone (speed = 2) on "white" terrain.
- Steps: Let Dog cross into a colored zone ("light blue" or "light green") and then return to "white" terrain.
- Expected Result: Speed remains unchanged across colored zones (+= 0 / -= 0) and resets to 1 upon re-entering "white" terrain.

[TC-08] Pinguin Win & Tie Condition
- Preconditions: Race reaches x >= 450 (game.finish_line).
- Steps:
  1. Let Pinguin cross x = 450 alone.
  2. Simulate Pinguin and another racer crossing x = 450 on the same frame.
- Expected Result:
  1. Loop terminates and displays "The winner is: Pinguin".
  2. Loop terminates and displays "It's a tie between: ..., Pinguin".
